# Kpop social media

A multi-artist fandom social platform, designed to connect artists and fans through dedicated communities, social interactions, multimedia content, and fan engagement activities.

| Category | Technologies |
|---|---|
| Frontend | nextjs |

| Version Control | Git, GitHub |
| Development Tools | Postman |

### Development Approach
- Modular and maintainable code structure.
- Client-server architecture.
- RESTful API communication.
- Database-driven content management.
- Scalable design for multiple artist communities.
## 2. Project Overview

**Weverse – Fandom Social Platform** is a community-driven social networking platform that brings multiple artists and their fandoms together in one centralized ecosystem.

The platform focuses on providing dedicated spaces where fans can follow their favorite artists, participate in communities, share content, interact with other fans, and stay updated on artist activities.

### Main Objectives

- Build a centralized platform supporting multiple artists and fandom communities.
- Provide dedicated spaces for fans to interact and share content.
- Enable users to follow artists and receive community updates.
- Support social engagement through posts, comments, and reactions.
- Provide event and concert information.
- Develop a scalable foundation for multimedia content and user-generated interactions.

## 3. Core Features

### Artist & Community Management
- Browse and discover multiple artists.
- View artist profiles and information.
- Create and manage dedicated artist communities.
- Join and participate in communities.

### Posts & Notices
- Publish community posts.
- Share announcements and important notices.
- Browse posts within artist communities.
- Support multimedia content in posts.

### Social Interactions
- Comment on community posts.
- React to posts and other user-generated content.
- Interact with other fans.
- Track engagement activities.

### Media Management
- Support images and multimedia metadata.
- Associate media content with posts and communities.
- Manage media references and related information.

### Events & Concerts
- Display upcoming artist events.
- Provide concert and event information.
- Associate events with relevant artists and communities.

### User Management
- User authentication and account management.
- User profiles.
- Community membership management.
- User-generated content management.
